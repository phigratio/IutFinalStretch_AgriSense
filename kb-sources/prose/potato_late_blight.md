# Potato late blight: source-backed safety advisory

BARI publishes a dedicated farmer advisory for potato late blight (মড়ক রোগ) and lists it in its
official factsheet collection. When late blight is suspected, treat it as a time-sensitive disease:
seek the current BARI/DAE recommendation or a local extension officer before choosing a product or
dose. This KB entry deliberately does not invent a pesticide, concentration, or spray interval; those
details must come from the current advisory label and local registration guidance.

# Fertilizer recommendations for AgriSense crops

These are nutrient recommendations in kg nutrient/ha, not fertilizer-product weights. Select the
soil-analysis class before using a range. Do not silently convert nutrient values to urea, TSP, MoP,
gypsum, or zinc product quantities.

## Boro and T. Aman rice — pages 72–74

For the listed higher-yield Boro varieties (7.5 ± 0.75 t/ha), low soil tests call for N 117–174,
P 13–18, K 81–120, S 13–18 and Zn 1.4–2.6 kg/ha. Apply P, K, S and Zn basally. Split N into
three equal applications: basal or immediately after establishment, early tillering, and 5–7 days
before panicle initiation. On light soil, apply half the K basally and half with the last N split.

For common T. Aman varieties with a 5.0 ± 0.5 t/ha goal, low soil tests call for N 49–72, P 9–12,
K 45–66, S 7–9 and Zn 0.8–1.4 kg/ha. The guide points to the rice application method above.

## Wheat and maize — pages 75–76

For irrigated wheat (4.5 ± 0.45 t/ha), low soil tests call for N 81–120, P 21–30, K 61–90,
S 11–15, Mg 4–8, Zn 1.4–2.6 and B 1.1–2.0 kg/ha. Apply two-thirds N and all other nutrients
basally; apply the final third of N at 17–21 days after sowing after the first irrigation.

For hybrid maize (11.0 ± 1.1 t/ha), low soil tests call for N 161–240, P 37–54, K 91–135,
S 25–36, Mg 11–15, Zn 2.1–4.0 and B 1.1–2.0 kg/ha. In Rabi, apply one-third N and all other
nutrients at sowing in covered furrows; split the remaining N between 50–55 days after sowing and
tasselling. The guide says Kharif doses may be reduced by 30%.

## Lentil and mustard — pages 83–87

For lentil (2.0 ± 0.2 t/ha), low soil tests call for N 19–27, P 13–18, K 17–24, S 9–12,
Zn 1.1–2.0, B 0.7–1.2 and Mo 0.5–0.8 kg/ha. Apply all fertilizer basally. If Rhizobium inoculum
is available, use 50 g/kg seed or 1.5 kg/ha and omit N fertilizer.

For mustard (Brassica juncea/napus, 2.0 ± 0.2 t/ha), low soil tests call for N 61–90, P 17–24,
K 51–75, S 17–24, Mg 4–6, Zn 1.4–2.6 and B 0.6–2.0 kg/ha. Apply half N and all other
nutrients basally; top-dress the remaining N at flower initiation, about 25 days after sowing.

## Potato and onion — pages 91 and 107

For potato (30 ± 3 t/ha), low soil tests call for N 91–135, P 21–30, K 91–135, S 11–15,
Mg 6–10, Zn 3–4 and B 0.81–1.6 kg/ha. Apply organic fertilizer, P, S, Mg, Zn, B, and half N
and K basally. Side-dress the remaining N and K at 30–35 days after planting during earthing up.

For winter onion (20 ± 2 t/ha), low soil tests call for N 61–90, P 21–30, K 51–75, S 21–30,
Zn 1.6–2.6 and B 0.8–1.4 kg/ha. Apply organic fertilizer, P, S, Zn, B and half N and K basally;
split the remaining N and K at 25 and 50 days after planting for bulb production.

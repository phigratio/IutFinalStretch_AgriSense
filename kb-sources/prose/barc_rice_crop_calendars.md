# BARC rice crop calendars: Aus and Aman

This is a conservative transcription of two Bangla crop-calendar screenshots issued by the
Bangladesh Agricultural Research Council (BARC) with AFACI support. Only clearly legible values
are included. The original PNG files are retained beside `SOURCES.md` for visual audit.

## Aman rice calendar

BARC's Aman calendar spans June through December. For seed preparation, soak selected seed for
48 hours before pre-germination. For modern high-yielding varieties, the calendar gives a seedbed
sowing window from 15 June to 15 July. For photosensitive varieties it gives 20 July to 30 July.

Transplant seedlings at about 25–30 days old. The displayed spacing is 20–25 cm between rows and
15–20 cm between hills, using 2–3 healthy seedlings per hill.

The fertilizer table shown for Aman gives, per hectare: urea 172 kg, TSP 52 kg, MoP 82 kg and
gypsum 38 kg. The calendar says TSP, MoP and gypsum are applied during final land preparation.
For medium-duration varieties, divide urea into three portions: shortly after transplanting,
during active tillering, and 5–7 days before panicle initiation. These are calendar defaults;
soil-test recommendations should override them.

## Aus rice calendar

BARC's Aus calendar spans March through August. It shows a seed requirement of approximately
12–20 kg/ha for transplanted Aus and 30–50 kg/ha for broadcast/direct-seeded Aus. Soak seed for
48 hours before pre-germination.

For transplanted Aus, sow sprouted seed in the seedbed from 15 March to 20 April. The calendar
shows a direct-seeded Aus window from 15 March to 15 April. Transplant seedlings at approximately
20–25 days old, with 20–25 cm between rows and 15–20 cm between hills.

The fertilizer table shown for Aus gives, per hectare: urea 127 kg, TSP 52 kg and MoP 60 kg.
TSP and MoP are applied during final land preparation. Urea is split rather than applied all at
once. These values are general calendar guidance and must yield to a soil test or newer BARC
fertilizer recommendation.

## Safety and precedence

The screenshots also cover irrigation, weed, disease and insect management, but small treatment
text is not transcribed here because pesticide names and doses require exact legibility. Consult
the original image or local extension guidance for those decisions. Numeric fertilizer advice from
the current BARC FRG-2024 and a field soil test takes precedence over this older calendar poster.
